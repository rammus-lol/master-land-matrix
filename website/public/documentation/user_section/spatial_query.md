# Data Extraction

Now you gave us some shapes, you can ask us which deals are registered inside of it.
For that  you just have to click on Extract Land Matrix Data.
After a certain amount of time you will receive a visualization of transaction,
based on their contract size and level of accuracy.

### Include inaccurate data mechanism explanation

Some records are placed at the geometric center of an administrative region or even the country. 
This button, enabled by default, includes them, but you can choose to exclude them from your extraction.

### When extraction end

When you got points and polygons on the map, you can click on it will provide a popup
with some resume of the dataset.
The bottom left button (the one with the orange polygon), provide a
display manager telling you how much of each layer you have fetch.

the layers are divided between : 
- administrative regions
- Deals with a shape provided
- Deals without a shape provided and a high confidence in the location accuracy.
- Deals without a shape provided and a low confidence in the location accuracy.

You can toggle each layer displaying in it as well.
