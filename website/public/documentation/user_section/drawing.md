# Drawing Tool

This section will learn you to draw efficiently shape in our app

## Polygon

Polygon option let you draw any non-circular shape.<br>
The only obligation is that your first point and your last point have to coincide. 

All you have to do is click somewhere,  (left or right click isn't important)
and close the shape by click again on the first point of the shape.<br>

If you want to cancel the last point you have drawn you can click on "Undo".

In the other hand Clear clean the entire canvas.

## Circle

Use this tool to manually draw a circle. 
Click to set the center point, then moove your mouse and 
click again to define the radius.

## Known Point

The last option allow you to draw a circle by setting a point knowing its GPS coordinates
and a radius in meter.
when you click on the known point button you have to scroll down  to discover the forms.

For Circle and Known point undo erase last drawn circle

## Edit shapes

This button activates Edit Mode. In this mode, you can select a shape by clicking on it. Clicking on its border allows you to:

- Adjust the radius (for circles).

- Move or add a vertex (for polygons).

Once a shape is selected, you can remove it by pressing the Delete or Backspace key.
